**Subject:** Permission to include Microban levels in OpenSoko (free, open-source)

Hello Mr. Skinner,

I'm building OpenSoko, a free, open-source Sokoban-inspired game that runs in the browser
(source: https://github.com/raulesme/opensoko, code under the MIT licence, no ads, no charge).

Your Microban collection is a great introduction to the game, and I'd like to include it as
a starter level set. Would you allow me to bundle the Microban levels in the game and its
public repository, with full credit to you (your name, the collection name and a link to
your Sokoban page shown on the level-select screen and in the credits file)?

Specifically I'd like to know:

1. May I distribute the levels unmodified (converted only to our text/JSON format) inside the
   game and the GitHub repository?
2. Do you want a particular credit line or link?
3. Are there any conditions (non-commercial only, no modifications, etc.)?

The levels would stay under your terms and would not be relicensed under MIT. I'd record
your reply in the repository so the permission is documented.

Thank you for your time, and for the levels.

Best regards,
